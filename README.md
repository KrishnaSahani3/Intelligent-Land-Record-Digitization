# 🏡 LandSure AI

### AI-Powered Land Record Digitization, Validation & Dispute Reduction Platform

> **SIH 2026 – Problem Statement 26018**

LandSure AI is an AI-powered platform designed to modernize land-record management by digitizing legacy documents, extracting information using OCR, validating records, detecting inconsistencies, and supporting transparent land-dispute resolution.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Land records are the backbone of property ownership, taxation, land acquisition, dispute resolution, and infrastructure planning. However, a large amount of historical land data still exists in handwritten registers, scanned documents, cadastral maps, and legacy PDFs.

**LandSure AI** provides a unified digital platform that converts these records into structured, searchable, and verifiable information.

The platform combines:

- 📄 AI-powered OCR
- 🔍 Land-record search
- ✅ Automated record validation
- ⚠️ Conflict & duplicate detection
- 🗺️ Cadastral GIS / BhuNaksha-style visualization
- 👤 Human verification
- ⚖️ Land-dispute reporting and tracking
- 📊 Analytics and risk advisory
- 📝 Mutation history
- 🔐 Audit trails

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional land records face several challenges:

- Historical records are stored in physical or scanned formats.
- Handwritten documents are difficult to search and process.
- Manual data entry can introduce errors.
- Different records may contain conflicting ownership or area information.
- Duplicate or inconsistent records can contribute to land disputes.
- Citizens often lack a transparent way to track complaints.
- Officers require multiple sources to verify a land record.

These challenges can make land administration slower and increase the difficulty of resolving disputes.
